A review copy's opening and end cards carry a QR code, with the cuttingfloor mark in the middle, that opens the video's release page on cuttingfloor.ai. Its owner signs in there, sees the price and their credits, removes the watermark and downloads the clean copy; anyone else who scans a shared video finds nothing to spend. Watermarked copies made before this get the code on their next download.
