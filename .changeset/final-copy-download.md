The player and editor download a watermarked export as the final copy by default: Download final removes the watermark for its price (shown beside the user's credits) and saves the clean file, or the menu switches to the free review copy, with a short "What's the difference?". Too few credits defaults to the review copy with a Get credits link. Saves from these buttons are counted apart (`from_view` on admin API download days, 1.9.0).
