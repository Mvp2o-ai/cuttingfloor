Hosted plans are enforced as sold. Each plan's render minutes are counted per job, and at the limit new jobs wait for the renewal (editing and previews keep working; nothing is billed past the price). Jobs at once, the largest export and judgments a day follow the plan. Account checks fail closed, a failed payment keeps working for 7 days, and account email (welcome, trial ending, minutes at 80% and 100%, payment failed) comes from support@cuttingfloor.ai. Self-hosted servers are unchanged: no plan, no limits.
