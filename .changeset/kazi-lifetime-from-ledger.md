Lifetime for Kazi members comes from the shared ledger: an account Kazi calls unmetered (Kazi lifetime or staff) has cuttingfloor's lifetime, 900 credits a month, and never spends from the ledger. Using cuttingfloor through Kazi, or a key Kazi's app asks for, no longer gives lifetime by itself; accounts tagged before keep it. `CUTTINGFLOOR_LIFETIME_CLIENTS` is no longer read.
