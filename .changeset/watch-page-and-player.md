**Share and show exports.** `export.video` and `media.get` now return a `page_url`: a page that plays the file in any browser with no sign-in, to give a person to watch or share. It lasts as long as the signed download (24 hours by default). In clients that support MCP Apps, `export.video` and `media.get` results also play in the chat: the player follows a running export and plays it when it's done.
