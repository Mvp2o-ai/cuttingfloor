media.listen says what it can't hear: its model learned everyday sound events, so inside music it hears "music", not drums, instruments or single hits (on a drum bed "music" scores 1 and every drum query 0). The description and the result's new `note` say so and point to media.analyze beats and create.stems; the description also says a short sound can score higher listened to over a short range around it.
