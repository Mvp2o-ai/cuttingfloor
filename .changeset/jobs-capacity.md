---
'cuttingfloor': minor
---

Jobs no longer hold the server when they stall, and agents can wait for them and find them. A running job whose progress stops for its stall limit (`CUTTINGFLOOR_STALL_SECONDS`, default 300, longer for long media) ends with a `stalled` error and frees its slot; `job.cancel` stops a job within seconds, killing its engine's whole process group. Work queues by the resource it holds: `create.stabilized` no longer waits in the heavy-model slot, and lip sync, seek and look wait in a lane of their own. One extra slot (`CUTTINGFLOOR_SHORT_JOBS`) is kept for short jobs (up to `CUTTINGFLOOR_SHORT_JOB_SECONDS` of media), so an 8 s transcript doesn't wait behind long ones. `job.get` takes `wait_seconds` (up to 50) and answers as soon as a job moves, gives `seconds_left` and `starts_in_seconds` when progress allows, and `result: summary` cuts long lists. Every job names what it is `for` (media, timeline, kind, preset). `job.list` filters by `tool` and `media_id`, and now leaves results out by default (`result: summary` or `full` to include them). `media.get` names the job importing or making the media, and `media_not_ready` hints say to wait for it with `wait_seconds`. Transcripts report progress as whisper runs.
