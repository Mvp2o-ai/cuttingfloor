Scores: music made in layers, a new document beside the timeline. 28 score.* tools create, read, load, diff and edit one (tempo and meter maps, markers, parts, instruments and effects from a device registry, notes, notes and audio regions with mirrored copies, automation, sends), score.batch runs many edits in one call with $refs, and video://catalog/devices lists every instrument and effect with its params. Every position comes back in beats, seconds and frames, and every position argument takes seconds instead.
