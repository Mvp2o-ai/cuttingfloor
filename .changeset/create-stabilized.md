**`create.stabilized`: steady shaky video.** A new long-running tool makes a steadied copy of video media with vid.stab (one pass measures the camera shake, a second moves each frame against a smoothed path), as new ProRes media with the sound, ready for `clip.replace`. Options: `shakiness`, `smoothing`, `tripod`, and `edges` (zoom, adaptive zoom, black or kept edges).
