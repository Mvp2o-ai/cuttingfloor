Select, then act on the selection. create.cutout takes a region (a box and/or points at a moment) and follows that one item's exact outline through the video (EdgeTAM). clip.set_mask takes a matte (any media's alpha, such as that cut-out) as its selection, polygon points that take keyframes, and a mode: show, hide, or fill, which paints the selection over from its surroundings in every frame (LaMa).
