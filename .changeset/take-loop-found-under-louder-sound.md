score.record: the loop is found in a microphone take recorded over a speaker with a voice or room sound on top. The check compared the take and the loop in the speech band, where the performance and the room are loudest, and placed the loop wrongly (1.97 s out on a real take). Both are now divided by the recording's spectrum first, at a higher sample rate, so the bands where the loop stands clear decide the match.
