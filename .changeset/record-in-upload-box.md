Record in the upload box, on the page its link opens and on the upload page: record from the microphone, listen back, record again, send. Nothing leaves the device until Send, and the recording is staged exactly as a chosen file is. The box declares the microphone (`_meta.ui.permissions.microphone`); where a chat app does not grant it, the box says so and opens the upload page in the browser, which records to the same upload. media.upload's description says the box and the page record.
