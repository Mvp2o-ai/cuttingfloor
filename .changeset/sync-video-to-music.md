New skill, sync-video-to-music: how your AI cuts a video to a song (shots change on the beat or the bar, the end lands on the last hit), fits a song to a finished video (trimmed on whole bars, ending on a hit, faded out) and turns the music down while someone speaks. `skills.search` finds it for "sync video to music", "cut to the beat" and "fit music to video".
