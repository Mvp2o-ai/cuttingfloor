Credits only, pay as you go: subscriptions are gone. New accounts start with 10 free credits (accounts made before November 8, 2026 keep their 100), then buy packs ($5 for 25 credits up to $100 for 700) that never expire, in one click on a saved card or automatically when credits run low. Emails go out when credits run low and run out. New tool `account.get` returns the account's credits, warnings, top-up and the account page link. The account page shows credits left and puts uploading files right under them; uploads are counted on the site.
