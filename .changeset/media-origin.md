`media.get` and `media.list` say where each media came from (`origin`): the URL (without its query), path or upload (sent through media.upload, the account page or a data: URI) it was imported from, the export of which timeline and preset, or the create.* tool, its job and the media it was made from. `media.get` also gives `created_at`.
