**A proper in-chat player.** The player that shows `export.video` and `media.get` results in MCP Apps clients is rebuilt. It has its own controls: a scrubber that previews the frame under the pointer, 5-second skips, frame-by-frame stepping, a switch to timecode, speed, loop, volume, picture in picture, full screen and the usual keyboard keys. While an export runs, the whole frame shows a progress ring with the percentage and elapsed time instead of a line of text. A soft glow from the video fills the bars beside portrait video. Under the picture are the file's size, length, frame rate and when its link expires, with Copy link, Open and Download. The card paints its own background, so its text reads in light and dark chats.
