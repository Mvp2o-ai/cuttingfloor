Music is made here: score.render turns a score, or a range of it, into audio media for a timeline, with its loudness, any clipped samples, the parts that sounded, where each bar starts and, with tail_db, where the sound ends. The same score and range give the same audio. The engine is cf-music (Tracktion Engine, in the image): a sampler that plays any media, a synth, 18 named instruments and four drum kits (MuseScore General SoundFont, recorded so the loudest note peaks at -12 dBFS), and nine effects with real filter slopes and a true-peak limiter. The server's instructions name the score tools in place of "Music isn't made".
