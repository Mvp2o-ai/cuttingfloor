Two new tools on the hosted service. claim_free_credits records that the user's free credits were claimed, with one sentence on what they want to do with video, and returns their credit balance; it is safe to call any number of times, never changes a balance, and editing never depends on it. contact_support sends the cuttingfloor team a message about anything that failed, couldn't be done, or would help, and always answers with an acknowledgement.
