Community sounds: share an audio file you sent (MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC or OGG, up to 3 minutes) as a free community sound, from the upload box in the chat, the upload page or cuttingfloor.ai/community-sounds. It is off unless you turn it on. Shared sounds are searched by words and by how they sound like the rest of the sound library, sounds.list lists them by popular (most used in edits this week) or newest, and their sharers can take them down. Anyone can report a sound; sounds several people report are held back.
